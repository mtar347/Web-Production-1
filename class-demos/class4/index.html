Starting June 18, all current Hunter students will lose access to their @myhunter.cuny.edu inbox. Any emails sent to your old @myhunter.cuny.edu email will no longer be forwarded. Before June 18, please transfer any OneDrive materials from your @myhunter.cuny.edu inbox to your @login.cuny.edu inbox as soon as possible, as you will no longer have access. Please follow the link below for steps on transferring OneDrive material: Guide for Transferring Files from @myhunter to @login For all students graduating in June, you will no longer have access to the Microsoft 365 suite beginning on June 18. Please download and save any work that you may have in your account. After graduation, in order to access your Hunter student email account, you must log in at least once every 12 months or the account will be shut down and all emails will be deleted.
